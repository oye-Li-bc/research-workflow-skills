# Sources and redistribution

This project rewrites workflows developed in the maintainer's local research
sessions and Hermes customizations into portable, narrowly scoped skills.
It is not an export of the installed skill collection.

- Hermes is maintained by [Nous Research](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent).
  The local upstream checkout's LICENSE identifies it as MIT, copyright
  2025 Nous Research. Built-in skills, authentication helpers, local profiles,
  and session logs are not included here.
- The Work-side workflows `paper-download-arxiv-paper-source` and
  `paper-to-beamer` informed the requirements for source handling and slides.
  Their local copies did not establish redistribution rights for all referenced
  resources. Their text, scripts, and referenced university theme are not copied.
- The public skills and helper programs in this repository are new rewrites.
  `catalog.json` records the workflow lineage of each skill. A lineage entry is
  not a claim that all original material was written by the maintainer.
- No paper sources, figures, pretrained weights, external templates, or fonts
  are bundled. Their rights remain with their respective authors.

Using a workflow locally does not establish ownership of the underlying skill.
Contributors must document the origin and license of any material they import.
